Nine-point spatial calibration

Calibrate horizontal direction, vertical direction, and vergence distance together from nine known targets. You can capture fixations manually or import a CSV containing each target’s location and fixation ID.

Columns: name,horizontal_angle_deg,vertical_angle_deg,distance_m,fixation_id. Each ID must identify a different fixation in the loaded recording.
Load a recording to inspect spatial gaze direction.

The nine-point fit automatically replaces the three-point vergence fit while active. Its corrected depth drives the vergence readout and overhead convergence point. Original optical-axis readings and video gaze pixels remain unchanged. Use the same baseline head position throughout calibration; validate on additional targets.
