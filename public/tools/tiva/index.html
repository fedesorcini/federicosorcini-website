How TIVA works

Convert each exact p-value into an equivalent Z-score. Find their sample variance, V, using divisor k − 1. Compute χ² = (k − 1)V and a lower-tail probability with k − 1 degrees of freedom.

A low p-value indicates unusually little between-study variation under the reference model. It does not establish misconduct or replication failure.

Choose focal tests independently of their significance and include all eligible experiments. Ideally use comparable tests on independent samples.
